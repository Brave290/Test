About Phantom Creative

Where creativity meets strategy, and vision becomes reality.

Our Story

Founded in 2020 by Akanji Mus'ab, Phantom Creative emerged from a passion for pushing creative boundaries. What started as a solo design venture has grown into a collective of visionary artists dedicated to crafting visual identities that resonate.

We believe that great design is more than aesthetics—it's about telling stories, evoking emotions, and creating connections. Every project we undertake is approached with fresh eyes, strategic thinking, and unwavering commitment to excellence.

Today, we're proud to have partnered with over 200 businesses across industries, helping them transform their brands into memorable experiences.

"Design is not just what it looks like and feels like. Design is how it works."

— Steve Jobs

Our Mission

To empower businesses with exceptional design that communicates their unique story, builds trust, and drives growth in an increasingly visual world.

Our Vision

To become Africa's most trusted creative partner, setting new standards in design excellence and innovation.
